A better screenshot tool for Mac

Replace the built-in screen capture. Mark up every shot, and save smaller files automatically.

Download for Mac GitHub

Free and open source. Requires macOS 26 or later.

01

Replaces macOS screen capture

Take screenshots and recordings with the shortcuts you already know.

⌘⇧3Full screen⌘⇧4Area or window⌘⇧5Record

See every click and key

Recordings can show a ripple where you click and your pressed keys in the corner, so viewers can follow along.

02

Add text, arrows, and boxes

The editor opens right after you capture, so you can point things out and hide private details.

03

Smaller files, automatically

Screenshots save as WebP and recordings as WebM, with no extra steps.
You can also choose PNG, JPEG, MP4, or MOV.
