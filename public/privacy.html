PrintScreen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

PrintScreen works on your Mac. Your screenshots, recordings, and pressed keys are not sent to any server, and the app has no analytics or crash reporting.

Your captures

Captures are saved only to the folder you choose, or copied to your clipboard when you ask. WebM conversion runs locally with the ffmpeg installed on your Mac.

Pressed keys

When you enable this option, keys are shown on screen during a recording so they can appear in the video. PrintScreen does not save them separately.

Update checks

The About tab sends a request to github.com/morishin/printscreen/releases/latest to compare version numbers. GitHub receives ordinary request information such as your IP address.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording — to capture screenshots and videos
  • Accessibility — only to show pressed keys in recordings

You can review or revoke these in System Settings at any time.

This website

This site is hosted on Netlify and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may process standard request information such as your IP address.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
